# Better onboarding

## Context
The current game expects players to infer too much too early. Controls, goals, and trajectory feedback are not obvious on first contact, which raises the chance that players bounce before they understand the loop.

## Proposal
Tighten the first-run experience with clearer objective framing, more guided early prompts, and stronger feedback when the player fails or completes a step. Focus on helping players understand what they are trying to do and why the trajectory display matters.

## Open questions
- Should onboarding stay fully in-world inside the main scenario, or use more explicit tutorial overlays?
- How much text is acceptable before it starts slowing the game down?
- Which mistakes most need immediate explanation: crashing, drifting off course, or misusing time warp?
