# Mobile controls polish

## Context
Touch controls were a major usability gap for the game. Mobile needed a control scheme that preserved the desktop flight loop while remaining practical on a phone-sized screen.

## Proposal
Implement and refine mobile touch controls, then iterate on friction points such as gesture conflicts, menu interaction, and Safari-specific behavior like accidental double-tap zoom on stepper buttons.
