# Other ways of presenting visualizatin of trajetory lines

## Context

It's quite a pickle to cut a line soon enough to not let its circles to draw into each other and let multiple crosses when it's needed, cuz trajecotry is somehwat stable only. When that happens, line being draw layer by layer the dashed nature/feeling of the trajectoru line gets lost and becomes solid. Especially painful for long trajectories

## Proposal

To overcome draw layer onto layer let's make the trajectory being a solid dim color. Solid to not make a difference how many layers it has, and dim not to steal the attention to much, which was the original idea behind dashed line.

What color should it be ? how dimmed it should be ? Let's test multiple examples!
